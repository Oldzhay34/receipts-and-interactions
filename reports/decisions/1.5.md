# Decisions — 1.5 Ingredient Cleaning and Encoding

## 1. Modifier Removal List (`config/config.yaml -> ingredients.modifiers`)
- **Decision:** Remove high-frequency preparation, size, temperature, and state modifiers (e.g., `fresh`, `chopped`, `minced`, `large`, `extra virgin`, `to taste`) while preserving core ingredient nouns and keeping a safe-list (`_KEEP_S_WORDS`) for singularization.
- **Rationale:** Reduces surface-level vocabulary fragmentation (e.g., `fresh garlic`, `minced garlic`, `garlic` merge into `garlic`) without losing culinary identity.

## 2. Rare Ingredient Threshold (`RareIngredientFilter(min_count=10)`)
- **Decision:** Default `min_count = 10` configured in `config/config.yaml`. Fit is strictly restricted to training data inside the 1.9 pipeline (Constitution 4.1).
- **Rationale:** Removes typos, brand-specific strings, and one-off long-tail entries that inflate sparse matrix dimensionality and harm kNN distance quality while preserving the vast majority of ingredient occurrences.
- **Evidence (populate after Colab full-data run):**
  - Coverage table (`min_count` vs `vocab_size` & `occurrence_coverage_pct`): `<!-- src: outputs/ingredients/v1/rare_threshold_coverage.csv -->`

## 3. Sparse Encoding (`binary` vs `tfidf`) & Distance Metrics (`Jaccard` / `Cosine`)
- **Decision:** Support both `binary` and `tfidf` CSR sparse matrices stored atomically with `vocab` (`len(vocab) == matrix.shape[1]`). Use `JaccardDistance` and `CosineDistance` (never Euclidean distance on binary ingredient vectors; Constitution 4.5).
- **Rationale:** Binary ingredient vectors are high-dimensional and sparse. Under Euclidean distance, a 1-ingredient unrelated recipe can appear closer to a 3-ingredient query than a recipe sharing all 3 query ingredients plus 5 pantry items (proven in `tests/test_ingredients.py::test_euclidean_counterexample_on_sparse_binary_ingredients`).
