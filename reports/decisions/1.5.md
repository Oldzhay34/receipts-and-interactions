# Decisions — 1.5 Ingredient Cleaning and Encoding

## 1. Modifier Removal List (`config/config.yaml -> ingredients.modifiers`)
- **Decision:** Remove 45 high-frequency preparation, size, temperature, and state modifiers (e.g., `fresh`, `chopped`, `minced`, `large`, `extra virgin`, `to taste`) while preserving core ingredient nouns and keeping a safe-list (`_KEEP_S_WORDS`) for singularization.
- **Rationale:** Reduces surface-level vocabulary fragmentation without losing culinary identity.
- **Evidence:**
  - Across **231,637** recipes, unique ingredients reduced from **14,942** to **12,402** (**2,540** removed, **17.0%** reduction) <!-- src: outputs/ingredients/v1/normalization_summary.parquet -->.
  - Top merged canonical examples: `chicken breast` (22 variants), `red pepper` (20), `potato` (18), `shrimp` (17), `tomato` / `pepper` / `mushroom` (15 each), `onion` (13) <!-- src: outputs/ingredients/v1/merged_variants.parquet -->.

## 2. Rare Ingredient Threshold (`RareIngredientFilter(min_count=10)`)
- **Decision:** Set `min_count = 10` in `config/config.yaml`. Fit is strictly restricted to training data inside the 1.9 pipeline (Constitution 4.1).
- **Rationale:** Eliminates 62.03% of the long-tail vocabulary (typos, brand names, one-off strings) while retaining **98.95%** of all ingredient occurrences, improving sparse kNN efficiency and distance quality.
- **Evidence** <!-- src: outputs/ingredients/v1/rare_threshold_coverage.parquet -->:

## 3. Sparse Encoding (`binary` vs `tfidf`) & Distance Metrics (`Jaccard` / `Cosine`)
- **Decision:** Support both `binary` and `tfidf` CSR sparse matrices stored atomically with `vocab.json` (`len(vocab) == matrix.shape[1] == 4,709`). Both full-catalog matrices have shape **(231,637, 4,709)** with **2,068,932** non-zero entries (`float32` CSR) <!-- src: outputs/ingredients/v1/manifest.json -->. Use `JaccardDistance` and `CosineDistance` (never Euclidean distance on binary ingredient vectors; Constitution 4.5).
- **Rationale:** Binary ingredient vectors are high-dimensional and sparse (~99.81% sparsity). Under Euclidean distance, a 1-ingredient unrelated recipe can appear closer to a 3-ingredient query than a recipe sharing all 3 query ingredients plus 5 pantry items (proven in `tests/test_ingredients.py::test_euclidean_counterexample_on_sparse_binary_ingredients`).
- **QueryVectorizer & Distance Verification:** Sample query `["Fresh Garlic", "Chopped Onions", "Olive Oil", "Dragon Fruit Dust"]` returned unknown `['dragon fruit dust']`, with min Jaccard distance **0.625** and min Cosine distance **0.3876** on the first 1,000 recipes (`notebooks/05_ingredients.ipynb`).
